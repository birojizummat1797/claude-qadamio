# So‘rovnoma bonusini qanday e’lon qilish — tadqiqot (2026-10-07)

Savol (Founder): bonusni boshida aniq aytamizmi yoki "sizni bonus kutadi" deb sirli qoldiramizmi?

## Dalillar
- Rag‘bat (incentive) javob berishni oshiradi: pul rag‘bati javob ehtimolini ~2 baravar, pulsiz rag‘bat ~20% oshiradi (Edwards va b., Cochrane meta-tahlili). Onlayn so‘rovnomalar bo‘yicha dalil kamroq. ([Cochrane](https://cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.MR000008.pub5/information/en), [GESIS](https://www.gesis.org/fileadmin/admin/Dateikatalog/pdf/guidelines/incentives_pforr_2016.pdf))
- Amaliy tavsiya: rag‘batni **ochiq va oldindan** aytish; noaniq kirish matni javob berishni pasaytiradi. ([Pointerpro](https://pointerpro.com/blog/average-survey-response-rate/))
- Marketing (lead magnet): **aniq natija** va'da qilgan taklif noaniqdan kuchli ("bepul qo‘llanma" < "10 ta tayyor g‘oya"); bonus darhol berilishi kerak. ([Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/uk/tutorials/what-is-lead-magnet/), [Claspo](https://claspo.io/blog/what-is-a-lead-magnet-and-how-to-create-one-that-drives-conversions/))
- "Noaniq mukofot" effekti (Shen, Fishbach, Hsee, 2015): mukofot **miqdori** noaniq bo‘lsa (masalan, $1 yoki $2), odamlar ko‘proq harakat qiladi. Lekin bu laboratoriyada, ishonchli tashkilot bilan; begona odamdan kelgan sirli "bonus" Telegram’da spam/firibgarlikka o‘xshab ishonchni pasaytirishi mumkin [taxmin]. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2015/03/09/391795455/how-do-you-get-people-to-work-harder-keep-the-reward-a-secret), [The Decision Lab](https://thedecisionlab.com/biases/motivating-uncertainty-effect))
- Rag‘bat ma’lumot sifatiga ta’sir qiladi: mavzuga qiziqqanlar ko‘proq qatnashadi. ([Survey Practice](https://www.surveypractice.org/article/122846-incentive-impact-on-data-quality-sample-composition-and-respondents-topic-interest)) Bizning bonus kasb mavzusida — natijada **kasb muammosi bor odamlar ko‘proq qatnashadi**; bu "kerakmi?" degan 1-maqsad natijasini biroz oshirib ko‘rsatishi mumkin — tahlilda hisobga olinadi.
